Mammut is a fediverse client for people who want to read their timeline and be done with it.

Built on Moshidon and the official Mastodon Android app, with a reading-first redesign on top:

CATCH UP
A bounded view of what you have not seen: posts since your last catch-up, capped at 24 hours and 200 posts, grouped by author in readable cards, with an honest ending.

READING ROOM
Conversations with a factual count of posts and people, side-branch markers, and an Unroll mode that lays a self-thread out in order.

COMPOSER 2.0
Drafts that survive leaving the app, threads with numbered segments, and scheduling with a list of what goes out next.

THE CAPTION TRACK
Attaching a photo fills its description from the file's own metadata and shows an editable field under each image.

SEARCH OPERATOR CHIPS
has:media, has:link, has:poll, is:reply, is:sensitive, from:me and in:library, with a full syntax reference.

Plus FAB navigation with a live unread counter, per-timeline filters, media blur, hidden domains, UnifiedPush notifications, and every feature Moshidon already offers.

Mammut is free software under the GPL-3.0. It is an independent fork, not affiliated with Mastodon gGmbH or the Moshidon project. If you enjoy it, please donate to Moshidon's developer, whose work this is built on.
